## Creating a team workspace

Workspace switcher → **New workspace**. Team workspaces are available on the Team plan (up to 10 members) and Enterprise.

## Roles

Viewer (read), Editor (edit, run, deploy), Admin (members, integrations), Owner (billing, delete). Details in [getting started](/docs/getting-started#workspaces).

## Inviting someone

Workspace → **Members** → **Invite** with an email and role. The invitee gets an email and must sign in with that address. Invitations expire; resend from the same page.

## Changing roles and removing members

Admins and owners change roles from the Members page. Removing a member revokes their access immediately; their past activity is kept in the log.

## Transferring ownership

The current owner can promote an admin to owner; there is exactly one owner per workspace.

## Working on the same file

Presence shows who has a file open. Simultaneous edits are not merged; the last save wins. Coordinate on shared files or use snapshots to recover.
