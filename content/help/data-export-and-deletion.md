## Exporting a project

Project menu → **Download** produces a zip of the current files. Snapshots can be downloaded the same way from the History panel. Pushing to GitHub is another full export.

## Exporting account data

Email [privacy@codexa.app](mailto:privacy@codexa.app) from the address on the account. We send a machine-readable export of profile, workspace memberships, project metadata, activity and billing records within the window stated in the [privacy policy](/privacy).

## Deleting a project

Owners and admins can delete a project. It is soft-deleted and purged after the retention window in [data retention](/data-retention).

## Deleting a workspace

The owner can delete a team workspace after cancelling its subscription. All projects in it are deleted.

## Deleting your account

Account settings → **Delete account**. Transfer ownership of any team workspaces first. The personal workspace and its projects are deleted; invoices are retained as required by law.

> Review: The retention periods and legal obligations referenced here require legal review before publication.
