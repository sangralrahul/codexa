## Channels

- **Support form**: [/support](/support) creates a ticket tied to your account.
- **Email**: [support@codexa.app](mailto:support@codexa.app).
- **Bugs and feature requests**: [/report-bug](/report-bug) and [/request-feature](/request-feature).
- **Security**: [security@codexa.app](mailto:security@codexa.app) or [/vulnerability-reporting](/vulnerability-reporting). Please do not file security issues as public bugs.
- **Sales**: [sales@codexa.app](mailto:sales@codexa.app) for Team and Enterprise.

## What to include

- The workspace and project id (from the URL).
- The error code shown (for example `quota_exceeded`) and the time.
- For preview or build problems, the relevant lines from the Logs or Deploy panel.
- For billing, the invoice number.

## Response times

Codexa is built by a small team. Tickets are answered in the order received; Team and Enterprise plans have priority support. We do not publish guaranteed response times at this stage.

## Status

Platform incidents are posted on [/status](/status).
